# Deep Research: How to Play

**The goal:** practise thinking and speaking clearly about a real topic from your Trimester II courses. You research it first, then speak on it.

1. **Pick your subject.** Random draws from any course, or choose AMM, MHR, FM, BAAI or OM.
2. **Set your timers.** Tap the gear icon: research 2, 3, 5 or 10 minutes; speech 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes.
3. **Spin.** Press Spin (or the Space bar). Spin again if you don't like the topic.
4. **Research.** Press Start research. Tick off the research angles, write notes (definitions, formulas, a real company, one example). You can pause.
5. **Speak.** When research time ends a beep sounds and the speech timer starts. Press Start speech early if you're ready. Speak out loud.
6. **Finish.** Press "I'm done" or let the timer run out. Did you define key terms, give one real example, and end with a clear takeaway?
7. **Go again.** Spin a new topic or retry the same one.

Notes are not saved: they disappear if you reload the page.
