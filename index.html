Deep Research: How to Play

The goal: practise thinking and speaking clearly about a real topic from your Trimester II courses. You research it first, then speak on it.

Rules

  1. Pick your subject. Choose 🎲 Random to draw from any course, or choose one: AMM, MHR, FM, BAAI or OM.
  2. Set your timers. Tap ⚙ and choose a research time (2, 3, 5 or 10 minutes) and a speech time (1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes).
  3. Spin. Press Spin (or the Space bar) and a topic is chosen at random. Don't like it? Spin again before you start.
  4. Research. Press Start research. The countdown begins.
    • Use the research angles shown as chips. Tick each one off as you cover it.
    • Write notes in the notes box: definitions, formulas, a real company, one example.
    • You can pause the timer at any point.
  5. Speak. When research time ends, a beep sounds and the speech timer starts. You can also press Start speech early if you feel ready. Speak out loud, using your notes if you need them.
  6. Finish. Press I'm done, or let the timer run out. Ask yourself:
    • Did I explain the key terms?
    • Did I give one real example?
    • Did I end with a clear takeaway?
  7. Go again. Spin a new topic, or retry the same one and aim to do better.

Good to know

  • Topics come only from the five course plans, so everything you practise is exam-relevant.
  • Your notes aren't saved. They disappear if you leave or reload the page.
  • Speak out loud, not in your head. It works better.
